Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions clients ask most

A shoot happens in four steps:

  1. We talk. The need, the place, the outfit, and the result you want to keep.
  2. The session. I guide you, without rushing, toward natural photos.
  3. Your gallery. You receive private access to see the photos and choose the ones included in the package.
  4. Delivery. I retouch your selection and send it in high resolution.

The usual time is 7 to 10 working days after the session. That time covers the edit, the retouching and putting the gallery online.

For delivery within 24 hours, the express option is €50.

For a portrait, personal use is included: social media, a CV, a personal website and prints for you.

Commercial use, for a company or a campaign, is set out in the quote. The photos remain my property: they cannot be resold or altered without agreement.

Bordeaux has beautiful settings for photographs. Here is what I recommend:

For urban professional portraits:

  • Place de la Bourse - Water mirror, classical architecture
  • Rue Sainte-Catherine - A lively urban atmosphere
  • Pont de Pierre - Views over the Garonne, sunsets
  • Grand Théâtre - Elegance and neoclassical architecture

For natural lifestyle portraits:

  • Jardin Public - Greenery, soft natural light
  • Quays of Bordeaux - Along the Garonne, a relaxed mood
  • Chartrons district - Authentic lanes, colored walls
  • Parc Bordelais - Nature, quiet, a variety of settings

I can also suggest places that fit your style and the season. Tell me what you prefer.

The weather can be unpredictable, and there are several ways through it:

  • Free reschedule: if the conditions are truly poor, we can move the session at no extra cost
  • Covered places: Bordeaux has many beautiful covered spaces (passages, arcades, covered markets)
  • Indoor shoot: we can switch to an indoor shoot with natural light
  • Light rain: sometimes a fine rain creates a unique, artistic mood

I watch the forecast in the days before your session and contact you if a change is needed.
